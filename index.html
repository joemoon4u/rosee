What I believe about skin

Good skin care isn’t a shopping list. It’s a relationship with the body you live in, and like any relationship it does best with attention, patience and honesty.

  • Listen before you layer

    Sleep, stress, seasons, hormones and habits all show up on your face. We begin with your story, not with a product list.

  • Protect the barrier first

    Your skin’s outermost layer keeps water in and irritants out. When it’s calm and comfortable, nearly everything else gets easier.

  • Fewer things, chosen well

    A beautiful routine can be three steps: cleanse, moisturise, protect. Anything beyond that has to earn its place on your shelf.

  • Sun care, softly and always

    Here in Australia, sunscreen belongs in your morning routine whenever the UV is 3 or above, not only on beach days.

  • Patience is an ingredient

    Most products need around six weeks to show you what they can do. We change one thing at a time, so we always know what’s working.

  • Every recommendation comes with a why

    You’ll know what each product does, why I chose it for you, and the signs that mean stop. No mystery and no pressure.

  • Knowing where advice ends

    I’m a skin consultant, not a doctor. If I notice a changing spot or anything that needs medical care, I’ll tell you gently and clearly, and suggest you see your GP or a dermatologist.

I don’t sell products, and no brand pays me. The only thing I’m invested in is your skin.
